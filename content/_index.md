I'm an Associate Professor of Physics & Astronomy at Vanderbilt University, where I direct [VIPER](https://as.vanderbilt.edu/viper/) — the Vanderbilt Initiative for Probes of Extreme Relativity. I chair the [NANOGrav](https://nanograv.org/) Collaboration and am one of six US scientists on the ESA–NASA LISA Science Team.

My group develops Bayesian and AI-driven methods for gravitational-wave data analysis, spanning pulsar timing arrays and LISA.
