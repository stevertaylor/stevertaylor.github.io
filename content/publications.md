## Book {#book}

**[Nanohertz Gravitational Wave Astronomy](https://www.routledge.com/Nanohertz-Gravitational-Wave-Astronomy/Taylor/p/book/9780367768621)**
S. R. Taylor · CRC Press / Routledge, November 2021 · 172 pp · ISBN 9780367768621

A comprehensive treatment of pulsar-timing-array searches for low-frequency gravitational waves, covering the derivation of the pulsar-timing response, source astrophysics, and Bayesian data-analysis techniques. Used in graduate courses worldwide.
